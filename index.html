Terms of Service

What the app is

KIYUO uploader is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the KIYUO studio. It uploads the studio’s own animated short videos to the owner’s own TikTok and YouTube accounts. On TikTok the video is sent to the account’s drafts (inbox); the owner reviews it and publishes it manually in the TikTok app.

Who may use it

Only the KIYUO studio owner, with their own accounts. The app is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in or post.

Content

  • Only original KIYUO content is uploaded: the studio’s own characters, artwork and voices.
  • Videos that use AI-generated material are labeled as AI-generated content on each platform.
  • The owner is responsible for every video and must follow the TikTok and YouTube terms and community guidelines.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, for the owner’s personal production workflow. Platform features and limits can change at any time.

Changes

These terms may be updated on this page; the effective date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Data the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (user.info.basic) to confirm which account is signed in, and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and read the owner’s channel details.
  • The video files and texts (title, description, hashtags) that the owner chooses to upload.

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner’s videos to the owner’s accounts and to confirm the upload. The app does not read other users’ data, messages, comments, followers or analytics.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner’s computer, in a local folder that is excluded from version control. Nothing is stored on a server operated by the app. Upload results (video ID and link) are kept in local project files.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok and Google as part of the upload itself.

Deleting access

Access can be removed at any time: in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and in Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. Deleting the local token files also ends access from the app.
